Training rules

Progressive overload

Reach the top of the rep range for every set with clean form and ~2 reps left, then increase by the smallest practical amount.

Deload when needed

If fatigue or performance drops persist, cut working sets by 30–50%, reduce loads and keep 3–4 reps in reserve for a week.

Warm-up

3–5 minutes easy movement, then ramp-up sets for the first compound lift. Warm-up sets do not count as work sets.

Quick cues for your main lifts.

Bench press

Feet planted, shoulder blades stable, controlled descent, consistent touch point. Use safety arms or a spotter.

Squat

Brace before each rep, keep balance over mid-foot, use a repeatable depth you can control, and set safeties.

Deadlift

Bar over mid-foot, brace, push the floor away, keep the bar close. Stop if form breaks down.

RIR — reps in reserve

RIR 3 means you estimate you could do 3 more clean reps. During the first two weeks, keep most sets around RIR 3.
